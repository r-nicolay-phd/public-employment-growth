Table S24. A zero-effect wage bill reproduces 5% of the same-year coefficient
Simulation under a calibrated null. The observed GDP, growth and controls of each country are kept and only the wage bill is replaced by a synthetic series with no effect on growth: ln W_sim,t = ln W_sim,t−1 + ε Δln nominal GDP_t + u_t, with u drawn with replacement from the country's own values of Δln W − ε Δln nominal GDP and the initial level equal to the observed wage bill in the country's first year; ε = 0.48 is the elasticity of Table S3 (variants 0.30 and 0.70); 1000 replications or more per measure. In each replication the regressions of Table 1 are re-estimated with the synthetic series: annual panel 1996–2023 with the controls of Table 1 and country and year fixed effects, in the Table 1 sample; five-year averages with period-mean controls or controls in t_p − 1 and country and period fixed effects, each in the sample of the observed specification. Share of observed: simulated mean divided by the observed coefficient. Panel C: placebo-numerator minus same-year coefficient in a stacked model with country × measure fixed effects, wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications); ρ is the correlation between the same-year ratio and the placebo ratio in the Table 1 sample. Panel D: coefficient on log(wage bill / trend GDP) in the decomposition of Table S3 divided by the sample mean of the wage bill over trend GDP, with a delta-method 95% CI (Driscoll-Kraay standard error); compare with T3. In the registered design the synthetic ratio is a random walk: in one replication its within-country SD is 4.03 points of GDP (observed: 0.98), its AR(1) coefficient 1.013 (observed: 0.970) and its maximum 78.4% of GDP. Panel E (post-hoc diagnostic, added after the registered result was seen and not used in any decision): the same null with the synthetic ratio anchored one step ahead on the observed ratio, ln(W/Y)_sim,t = ln(W/Y)_t−1 + (ε − 1) Δln nominal GDP_t + e_t, with e drawn from the country's values of Δln(W/Y) − (ε − 1) Δln nominal GDP; this removes the drift (within-country SD 1.382, AR(1) 0.869 in one replication). Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
