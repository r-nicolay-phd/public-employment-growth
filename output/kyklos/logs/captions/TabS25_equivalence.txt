Table S25. The data neither support nor rule out moderate wage bill effects
Equivalence: two one-sided tests at 5% (90% CI) for the wage bill lagged one year in the annual model of Table 1 (1996–2023, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors) and for T3 (five-year averages, wage bill and controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects, clustered standard errors). Equivalence at ±δ holds if the 90% CI lies within (−δ, δ); smallest bound: the smallest symmetric δ at which equivalence holds; MDE: minimum detectable effect at 5% and 80% power, (1.96 + 0.84) × SE. The growth effect of a 3-point cut is −3 × the 95% CI limits of T3. Influence: T3 re-estimated without the listed observations; transition economies: former Soviet Union, Central and Eastern Europe and the Balkans, periods starting before 1999; GFS data by 2000: countries whose general government wage bill starts in 2000 or earlier. Selection: means in the T3 sample of countries with GFS data by 2000 (early) and later (late), difference with standard errors clustered by country. All leave-one-out coefficients in Figure S8. Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
