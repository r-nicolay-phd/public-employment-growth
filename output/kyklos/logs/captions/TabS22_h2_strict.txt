Table S22. Relative pay remains associated with growth when measured strictly before the period
Five-year averages 1979–2023, no microstates; growth of real GDP per capita on the WWBI components of the wage bill, with the controls in t_p − 1 of T3, country and period fixed effects and standard errors clustered by country. Registered: harmonized sample of Table 3 (wage bill and public employment from the same year, the one closest to t_p − 1, searching t_p − 1 to t_p − 4 and then t_p to t_p + 3). Strict sample: only pairs measured in t_p − 1 to t_p − 4 (168 country-periods before singletons are dropped; 63 of the 243 pairs fall outside this window, and 104 are not in t_p − 1). (b) Each component with the log total wage bill as a control (the registered column 1 of Table S6 gives the −1.68 coefficient of the relative wage in this form, in the harmonized sample; it is shown in the replication files). (c) Relative wage over trend GDP per worker (Hamilton). (d) Log WWBI public employment instrumented with log ILOSTAT public-sector employment; effective F of Montiel Olea and Pflueger (equal to the cluster-robust first-stage F with one instrument) and Anderson–Rubin confidence set. (e) WWBI adjusted public-private premium, the premium's own year restricted to t_p − 1 to t_p − 4. Coefficients with stars, p-values and 95% CIs from the wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications), except the IV column (normal distribution, cluster-robust). Romano–Wolf: step-down adjusted p of H2 within the seven hypothesis tests of Table S13, with the same 999 country draws; the strict-sample family replaces only the H2 test and does not change the registered verdict. Within-country SD: standard deviation of the regressor after removing country means, in the estimation sample. Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
