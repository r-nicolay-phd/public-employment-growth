Table S28. Smaller checks: recessions, anticipation, trend filters and dynamics
Recessions: annual model of Table 1 (1996–2023, controls of Table 1, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors), estimated separately for country-years with a negative or positive Hamilton output gap in t − 1 (log GDP minus Hamilton trend), instead of excluding years of negative growth (last row, as in Table S3). Anticipation: wage bill in the last year of period p (t_(p+1) − 1) on growth in period p, controlling for the wage bill in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects, clustered standard errors. Trend filters: correlation between the annual growth of each trend (one-sided HP, λ = 6.25; Hamilton, H = 2, k = 2) and current GDP growth, Table 1 sample. Dynamics: T3 with the lagged five-year growth rate (LSDV, Nickell bias not corrected) and with bias correction (Table A2). GFSM breaks: the IMF GFS files used do not report the methodological basis (GFSM 1986, 2001 or 2014) by country and year, so the check of Table S8, row 7, could not be done. Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
