Table 2. General and central government series give similar coefficients once the sample is common; federations and unitary countries do not differ significantly
Five-year averages 1979–2023, no microstates; growth of real GDP per capita on the general government wage bill (IMF GFS) or the central government wage bill (WDI) in t_p − 1, with the controls in t_p − 1 of T3 and country and period fixed effects. Columns 1–6 use one sample (country-periods with both series and all controls); column 7 uses all country-periods with the central government series. One inference for the whole table: 95% percentile confidence intervals and stars from the same paired country bootstrap (999 replications, the same draws for both series), p-value = twice the smaller share of replications on either side of zero. Federations: Forum of Federations; 12 federations in the estimation sample (13 before singletons are dropped), median general/central ratio in 2010–2019 = 4.08 (12 federations with both series). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
