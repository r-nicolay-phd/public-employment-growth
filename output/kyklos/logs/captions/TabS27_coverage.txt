Table S27. The general − central gap does not differ significantly between federations and unitary countries
Common sample of Table 2 (five-year averages, wage bill and controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects). Paired country bootstrap: in each of 999 replications countries are drawn with replacement and all coefficients are re-estimated on the same draw (fixed effects on the resampled country copies); 95% percentile CI; p-value = twice the smaller share of replications on either side of zero. Unitary countries are split by the median general/central wage bill ratio in 2010–2019 (above 1.5: 26 countries; at or below 1.5: 36 countries in the common sample). Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
