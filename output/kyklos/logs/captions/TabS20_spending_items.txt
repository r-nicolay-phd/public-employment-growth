Table S20. The sign of the same-year bias follows the elasticity of each spending item to GDP
Annual panel 1996–2023, no microstates; growth of real GDP per capita on each measure of the spending item, with the controls of Table 1 (lagged growth, lagged log GDP per capita, human capital, investment, openness, inflation, population growth, terms-of-trade growth), country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3), 95% CI in brackets; stars from the same standard errors. Each column uses one sample, common to its five measures. Items: general government wage bill (IMF GFS; reference, as in Table 1); general government final consumption (WDI); total general government expenditure (IMF GFS); social benefits and transfers (GFS share of expense × total expenditure); gross public investment (IMF Investment and Capital Stock Dataset, general government). Placebo: lagged ratio × (lagged real GDP / current real GDP) × exp(country mean real growth of the item, deflated by the GDP deflator). Bias: same-year minus lagged coefficient, in a stacked model with country × measure fixed effects, wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications) with bootstrap 95% CI. Elasticity: 100 × Δlog of the item on 100 × Δlog of GDP (nominal; real deflated by the GDP deflator), country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay 95% CI. Predicted sign (equation 1): negative if the elasticity CI lies below one, positive if above, zero if it contains one; the condition is confirmed if the bias CI agrees (below zero, above zero or containing zero). Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
