Table S21. With predetermined controls, first-year timing matters little for the five-year coefficient
Five-year averages 1979–2023, no microstates; growth of real GDP per capita (average of the period, or of t_p + 1 to t_p + 4) on the wage bill (general government, % of GDP), country and period fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country. All cells of panel A use one sample, the intersection of every cell (country-periods with all measures and both sets of controls). Period-mean controls: log GDP per capita in t_p − 1, human capital in t_p and period means of investment, openness, inflation, population growth and terms-of-trade growth (the conventional specification). Controls in t_p − 1: all controls in the last year of the previous period (T3). Panel B starts from the wage bill in t_p − 1 with period-mean controls and switches one control at a time to its t_p − 1 value. Coefficients with stars and 95% CIs from the wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). Reduction from t_p to t_p − 1 with controls in t_p − 1: 29.0%; with period-mean controls: 45.5%. Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
