Table S26. The annual amplification result is explained by timing
Upper panel: differences between the same-year interaction (government consumption × institutions) and the lagged or trend-GDP interaction in the replications of Table A3, estimated in stacked models with country × measure fixed effects and measure-specific controls and year effects; wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications), bootstrap 95% CI. Panel C: approximate replication (mean of six WGI indicators, centred; consumption in % of GDP; controls of Table 1); panel D: published specification of Mollick and Vianna (2024) with government effectiveness (log consumption; growth of real GDP). Mechanism: (a) coefficient on the interaction between nominal GDP growth and institutions (centred) in regressions of the nominal growth of government consumption or of the wage bill (100 × Δlog) on nominal GDP growth, institutions and their interaction, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors, 1996–2023; (b) cross-country correlation between the variance of the Hamilton output gap and mean institutions; (c) cross-country correlation between the standard deviation of the cyclical part of consumption/GDP (fitted values of a country regression of the ratio on the Hamilton gap) and mean institutions; for (b) and (c) the estimate is the correlation and the p-value tests it is zero. Lower panel: specification of Table A3, panel A (five-year averages, System GMM and fixed effects), with the institutional index and the period separated; the two indices have different scales (ICRG/QoG 0–1; WGI −2.5 to 2.5). Exploratory (pre-registered in the plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
