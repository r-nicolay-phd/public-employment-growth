Table A6 (new rows). Fiscal years: the same-year bias survives the exclusion and the realignment of fiscal years
Countries whose general government reporting year does not start in January according to the IMF World Economic Outlook database (October 2026, attribute 'start/end months of reporting year' of general government expenditure); in the Table 1 sample: EGY, HKG, IRN, LSO, MUS, NPL, RWA, SGP, THA. Whether the GFS wage bill of these countries is converted to calendar years is not reported in the GFS metadata and could not be verified. Excluded: Table 1 (annual and five-year rows, their models and controls) and T3 re-estimated without these countries. Realigned: for fiscal years starting between April and September (EGY, HKG, IRN, LSO, MUS, NPL, RWA, SGP), the calendar-year ratio is the month-weighted average of the two fiscal years that overlap the calendar year, assuming each fiscal year is labelled by the calendar year that holds most of its months; measures over trend GDP and five-year measures are recomputed from the realigned ratio. Annual rows: Driscoll-Kraay standard errors; five-year rows: standard errors clustered by country. Differences: measures (or full and restricted samples) stacked with measure- or sample-specific fixed effects, controls and year effects; wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the second plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
