Table A18 (new panel). Cumulative effects: the dynamic estimate is too imprecise to compare with the five-year estimate
Shocks (a), (b) and (c) of Table A18; annual panel 1996–2023, country and year fixed effects, two lags of the shock and of growth. Wage-bill response: change in the wage bill (% of GDP) from t − 2 to t + h on shock (a), dated t − 1; Driscoll-Kraay 95% CI. Multiplier: cumulative change in log real GDP per capita (×100, from t − 1, summed over horizons 0 to h) on the cumulative change in the wage bill (points of GDP, from t − 2 for shock (a) and from t − 1 for shocks (b) and (c), summed over 0 to h), instrumented by the shock (LP-IV, Ramey and Zubairy, 2018); Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (bandwidth 4) and normal 95% CI; shock (b) is a generated regressor and its CIs are percentiles of a country block bootstrap that redoes the purge (999 replications; its standard error is the interquartile range of the bootstrap draws divided by 1.349, because draws with a near-zero first stage produce extreme ratios). F: Kleibergen-Paap robust first-stage F (one instrument); values below 10 indicate a weak instrument, and the multipliers and their CIs are then unreliable. θ/5: coefficient of the change in log GDP per capita (×100) from t − 1 to t + 5 on the mean change of the wage bill over h = 0–5, instrumented by the shock, divided by 5 (delta-method CI); dividing by 6, the number of growth years, gives values five-sixths as large. T3: the five-year coefficient (−0.142) and its 90% CI. Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the second plan addendum).
