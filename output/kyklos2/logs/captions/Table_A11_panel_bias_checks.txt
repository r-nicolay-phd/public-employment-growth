All rows except the last two: T3 specification (growth of real GDP per capita, five-year averages, wage bill in t_p − 1, controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects) in the T3 sample, with the variable named in the row added. Output gap: 100 × (log real GDP − Hamilton trend, H = 2, k = 2) in t_p − 1. Growth of p − 1: lagged five-year average growth (LSDV, Nickell bias not corrected); rows with it lose the first period of each country. Negative-gap rows: the wage-bill coefficient after a positive gap, the interaction, and their sum. 2SLS: the ratio in t_p − 1 instrumented by the annual ratios in t_p − 2 and t_p − 3, country and period fixed effects, T3 controls, standard errors clustered by country; WRE wild bootstrap. Bootstrap: wild cluster bootstrap by country, Webb weights, 9,999 replications (p for T3 differs slightly from Table A9 because the bootstrap seed differs). T3, same observations: T3 re-estimated on the observations of the row. Annual rows: Table 1 model (1996–2023, controls of Table 1, country and year fixed effects) with the ratio in t − 1 over Hamilton trend GDP in t − 1; coefficient with wild cluster bootstrap by country. Gelbach (2016): contribution of each added variable to the change in the wage-bill coefficient, standard errors clustered by country. γ × δ: product of the within regressions of growth in p and of the ratio in t_p − 1 on growth in p − 1 (country and period fixed effects); γ_long × π: the coefficient on growth of p − 1 in T3 + growth of p − 1 times the coefficient of the wage bill in a regression of growth of p − 1 on the wage bill and the T3 controls, which equals the observed change; 95% intervals from a country-pairs bootstrap (999 replications).
