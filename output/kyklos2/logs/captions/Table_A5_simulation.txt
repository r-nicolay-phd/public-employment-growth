Table A5. A zero-effect wage bill anchored on the observed ratio reproduces 29% of the same-year coefficient
Simulation under the null that the wage bill has no effect on growth. Observed GDP, growth and controls are kept and only the wage bill ratio s = W/Y is replaced by a synthetic series. A (main): ln s_sim,t = ln s_obs,t−1 − (1 − ε) Δln Y_t + u_t, with Δln Y the observed growth of nominal GDP and u drawn with replacement from the country's own values of Δln s_obs + (1 − ε) Δln Y, recentred to zero within country. B: ln s_sim,t = μ_i + ρ (ln s_sim,t−1 − μ_i) − (1 − ε) Δln Y_t + u_t, with ρ = 0.870 (within-country AR(1) of the observed log ratio), μ_i the country mean and u from the country's recentred residuals; ε = 0.48. C: design of the earlier version of this table (random walk in ln W from the country's first observed year, u from Δln W − ε Δln Y, not recentred), rerun on the same grid of ε. 1,000 replications per ε and design. In each replication the annual regressions of Table 1 (1996–2023, controls of Table 1, country and year fixed effects, Table 1 sample) and T3 (five-year averages, controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects, T3 sample) are re-estimated with the synthetic series. Cells: mean of the simulated coefficients and, below, their 2.5–97.5 percentiles. Slope: OLS slope of the simulated mean on 1 − ε, with the 2.5–97.5 percentiles of the slope computed replication by replication. Sanity check (registered in the second plan addendum): in A, the slope of the same-year coefficient is negative with an interval that excludes zero, and at ε = 1 the mean is within 10% of the observed coefficient in absolute value with an interval that contains zero; the anchored design passes it. Within-country SD, AR(1) (pooled OLS on the lag) and correlation with the placebo ratio: means over replications, Table 1 sample. D: the same-year ratio replaced by its three log components (×100), estimated jointly; covariance and correlation of the numerator innovation with growth after removing country and year effects; regressions of 100 × Δln W on real growth, with country and year fixed effects; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3). The coefficient on nominal GDP growth in D is mechanically positive because real growth, the dependent variable, is part of nominal growth. Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the second plan addendum).
