Table S4. Distributed lags of the wage bill (annual panel) and leads (five-year panel)
Upper panel: annual panel 1996–2023, growth of real GDP per capita on the wage bill lagged one to five years, entered jointly, with country and year fixed effects and the controls of Table 1; coefficients and the sum with Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3) and delta-method confidence intervals; the sum is also tested by wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications) in the same model with standard errors clustered by country. The five lags are almost collinear (correlation above 0.98 between the first and the fifth lag and between the two denominators), so individual coefficients and their sum are imprecise and sensitive to the denominator. The exploratory row, added after the analysis plan, replaces the five lags by their average as a single regressor (common sample of both series). Lower panel: five-year averages, country and period fixed effects, predetermined controls of Table 2, standard errors clustered by country; the first row is equivalent to regressing growth in p − 1 on the wage bill in t_p; a non-zero coefficient would indicate anticipation (growth predicting the future wage bill). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
