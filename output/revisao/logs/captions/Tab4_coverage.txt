Table 4. General versus central government wage bill on the same sample
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, five-year average), 1979–2023. Columns 1–6 use the same country-periods for both measures (country-periods with the general government wage bill, IMF GFS, and the central government wage bill, WDI, both in t_p − 1, and the predetermined controls of Table 2); column 7 uses every country-period with the central government series. Federations follow a single definition, the Forum of Federations list (26 countries in the data); 15 federations have both series in 2010–2019, when the median ratio of the general to the central government wage bill is 3.41 in federations and 1.23 in unitary countries (76 unitary countries); 12 federations are in the conventional-specification sample. Country and period fixed effects; standard errors clustered by country. The difference between the general and the central government coefficients is estimated on 999 pairs-bootstrap samples drawn by country, with both equations re-estimated on each sample; percentile confidence interval and normal-approximation p-value. Microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
