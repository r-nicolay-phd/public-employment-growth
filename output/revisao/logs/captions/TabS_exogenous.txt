Table S10. Narrative wage-bill consolidations: local projections and first stage
Shock: wage-bill measures (Spending – Salaries) of the fiscal adjustment plans in Alesina, Favero and Giavazzi (2019), sum of unanticipated measures and of announced measures implemented in the year, % of previous-year GDP; positive values are cuts. 16 OECD countries, shocks 1978–2014. Cumulative change of the outcome from t − 1 to t + h on the shock, controlling for the non-wage spending and tax measures of the same plans and two lags of growth; country and year fixed effects; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3). With 16 countries, the wild cluster bootstrap p-value at h = 4 is exploratory. First stage: real change in the general government wage bill (IMF GFS, % of lagged GDP) on the same shock and controls, standard errors clustered by country; with one instrument the effective F equals the squared cluster-robust t statistic. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
