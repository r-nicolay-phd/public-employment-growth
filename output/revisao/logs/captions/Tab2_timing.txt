Table 2. Timing of the initial wage bill and predetermined controls
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, average over the years shown). t_p is the first year of period p and t_p − 1 the last year of the previous period. Columns 1–6: non-overlapping five-year averages, 1979–2023; column 7: three-year averages, 1979–2023; column 8: annual panel, 1996–2023, with lagged growth. Period-mean controls (conventional): log GDP per capita in t_p − 1, human capital in t_p and period means of investment, openness, inflation, population growth and commodity terms-of-trade growth. Predetermined controls: the same variables in t_p − 1 (in t − 1 in column 8). Column 5 drops investment, the mediator proposed in H4. Column 6 divides the wage bill by trend GDP from the Hamilton filter (H = 2, k = 2). Country and period fixed effects; standard errors clustered by country (t distribution with G − 1 degrees of freedom), except column 8 (Driscoll-Kraay, maximum lag 3, t distribution with T − 1 degrees of freedom). Wild cluster bootstrap p-values: Webb weights, 9,999 replications, clustered by country. The second panel restricts each specification to the conventional-specification sample (365 country-periods), keeping the country-periods that also have the variables of that column. Microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
