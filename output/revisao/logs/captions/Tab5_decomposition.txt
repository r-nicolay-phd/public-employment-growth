Table 5. Employment or relative pay? Decomposition of the wage bill
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, five-year average), 1979–2023 (WWBI data from 2000). Column 1: total wage bill (first year of the period) on the country-periods of the conventional decomposition (205 country-periods) that also have the first-year wage bill, with the conventional controls. Columns 2–7: the wage bill and public employment are taken from the same year, the one closest to t_p − 1 (searching t_p − 1 to t_p − 4, then t_p to t_p + 3); 243 country-periods have a pair, 139 exactly in t_p − 1 (column 4) and 104 in another year. log WageBill = log PubEmp + log RelWage holds exactly in the data, so the log coefficients are directly comparable. Columns 3, 6 and 7 use the same harmonized sample (log employment, log relative wage and the adjusted premium all available). Column 5 instruments log WWBI public employment with log ILOSTAT public-sector employment in the same year (country and period fixed effects, clustered errors); with one instrument the effective F of Montiel Olea and Pflueger equals the cluster-robust first-stage F. The premium is the WWBI adjusted public-private wage gap against all private employees, rescaled to 0.1 log point. Within-country SD: standard deviation of the regressor after removing country means in the estimation sample. Predetermined controls of Table 2; country and period fixed effects; standard errors clustered by country; wild cluster bootstrap with Webb weights and 9,999 replications. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
