Table S11. Bond check on a common sample and long-run estimators
Upper panel: five-year averages; the four estimators use the same country-periods (intersection of their own samples: 288 observations with the conventional timing and 306 with T3). The difference in countries between system GMM and fixed effects in the conventional specification came from 15 countries with a single observation (singletons), which fixed effects drop and pooled OLS and system GMM keep. Pooled OLS (clustered SE), fixed effects with country and period effects (clustered SE), two-step system GMM with collapsed instruments and Windmeijer-corrected SE, and the bias-corrected fixed-effects estimator of Breitung, Kripfganz and Hayakawa (2022) with robust SE, which assumes the regressors other than lagged growth are strictly exogenous. A consistent estimator of the coefficient of lagged growth should lie between the fixed-effects (downward-biased) and pooled OLS (upward-biased) estimates. Lower panel: annual panel 1996–2023, countries with at least 20 complete annual observations; ARDL models of growth on the lagged wage bill, investment, openness, inflation and population growth. PMG (common long run), MG and DFE estimated with xtpmg; Hausman test of PMG against MG: p = 0.721 (% of GDP) and 0.923 (% of trend GDP). CCE-MG (static), CS-ARDL(1,1) and CS-DL (xtdcce2) add cross-sectional averages (Pesaran, 2006; Chudik et al., 2016). Pesaran CD statistic on the residuals of the annual models of Table 1: -0.25 (p = 0.802) with the contemporaneous ratio and -0.20 (p = 0.840) with the lagged ratio. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
