Table S13. Hypothesis tests: preferred specifications, bootstrap and multiple-testing adjustment
Preferred specification of each hypothesis as registered in the analysis plan (commit 2182199, 17 October 2026). Wild cluster bootstrap: Webb weights, 9,999 replications, clustered by country. Romano-Wolf step-down p-values over the seven tests, from 999 bootstrap resamples of countries shared by all tests (each test re-estimated on every resample with fixed effects on the resampled country copies). Verdict rule: supported if the coefficient has the predicted sign, the bootstrap p-value is below 0.05 and the Romano-Wolf p-value is below 0.05; inconclusive if the sign is right and the bootstrap p-value is between 0.05 and 0.10, or below 0.05 without surviving Romano-Wolf; not supported otherwise.
