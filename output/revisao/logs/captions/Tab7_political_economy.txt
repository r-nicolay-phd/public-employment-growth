Table 7. Political economy of the wage bill
Panel A: annual panel 1996–2020 (DPI ends in 2020), real change in the general government wage bill (% of lagged GDP) on an indicator of an election in the year the constitutional term expires, with lagged growth, country and year fixed effects and standard errors clustered by country; wild cluster bootstrap with Webb weights and 9,999 replications. Democracies: V-Dem polyarchy ≥ 0.5; new democracies: 16 or fewer years of continuous democracy, an approximation of the first four elections (Brender and Drazen, 2005). With one instrument the effective F equals the squared cluster-robust t statistic. The second stage (growth on the instrumented wage bill change, with the fiscal balance and lagged growth) is reported through the Anderson-Rubin confidence set, which is robust to weak instruments; the instrument passes the pre-specified threshold (effective F ≥ 10) in 0 of the four samples. Panel B: cumulative response of 100 × log GDP per capita four years after a real wage bill shock of one percentage point of GDP, separately for shocks in predetermined election years and in other years; country and year fixed effects, two lags of the shock and of growth, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3). Panel C: five-year averages, preferred H1 specification (T3) with the wage bill in t_p − 1 interacted with each characteristic in t_p − 1 (centred at its sample mean), one model per row; standard errors clustered by country. Staggered subnational elections in federations: not executed (no public subnational election calendar in the project data). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
