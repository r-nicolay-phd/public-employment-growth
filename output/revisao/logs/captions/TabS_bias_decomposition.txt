Table S3. Decomposition of the contemporaneous-ratio bias
Dependent variable in panels A and B: growth of real GDP per capita (%). Panel A: annual panel 1996–2023 with country and year fixed effects, lagged growth, lagged log GDP per capita, human capital, investment, openness, inflation, population growth and commodity terms-of-trade growth; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3); same sample as the annual rows of Table 1, except the last row, which drops years with negative GDP per capita growth. The placebo numerator is the previous year's real wage bill grown at the country's average real growth of the wage bill, so the ratio varies with current GDP only through the denominator; its coefficient is the bias produced by the denominator alone. Y* is trend real GDP from the Hamilton filter (H = 2, k = 2); the two decomposition terms enter jointly and are in log units. Panel B: the rebound argument predicts a more negative coefficient when the lagged wage bill is divided by trend instead of actual GDP. Five-year rows: country and period fixed effects, conventional controls, standard errors clustered by country, common sample of the four five-year measures of Table 1. Panel C: annual log changes (×100) of the nominal wage bill on nominal GDP, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors; observations with absolute changes above 200 excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
