Table S7. Institutions, fiscal cyclicality and the measure of government size
Annual panel 1996–2019, growth of real GDP per capita on government size, institutions (mean of six WGI indicators, centred) and their interaction, with country and year fixed effects, lagged growth and the controls of Table 1; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3). Cyclicality (Frankel, Végh and Vuletin, 2013): country-specific slope of the log change in real government consumption on the output gap (two-sided HP), estimated over 1970–1995 (before the sample) or, as an exploratory check, over 1996–2023; entered as an interaction with consumption (centred). Correlation between pre-1996 cyclicality and average institutions across countries: -0.139 (74 countries); with 1996–2023 cyclicality: -0.120. In a regression of the change in real government consumption on the output gap, its interaction with institutions is -0.0750 (p = 0.4448). Last three rows: government consumption (WDI) in its own sample, consumption and total expenditure (IMF GFS) on the same country-years. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
