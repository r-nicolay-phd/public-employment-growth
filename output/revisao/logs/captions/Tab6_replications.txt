Table 6. Institutions and government size: replications with contemporaneous and predetermined measures
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%) in panels A–C; growth of real GDP (%) in panel D. Panels A and B follow the panel specification of Oto-Peralías and Romero-Ávila (2013, working-paper version INFER 2012/3): five-year averages, growth on government final consumption (% of GDP, WDI), institutional quality and their interaction, with initial log GDP per capita, secondary school enrolment and investment, and period effects. Their ICRG bureaucracy-quality index is proprietary; panel A uses the public ICRG quality-of-government index distributed by QoG (0–1, from 1984) and panel B the WGI government effectiveness estimate (from 1996), as specified in the analysis plan; legal origin, one of their excluded instruments, is not available and is dropped. System GMM: two-step, collapsed instruments, consumption, interaction and investment endogenous (lags 2 and deeper), income, enrolment and institutions predetermined (lags 1 and deeper), period dummies and the population dependency ratio as standard instruments; Windmeijer-corrected standard errors. Fixed effects: country and period fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country. Only the timing of government consumption changes across column pairs (period mean, first year of the period, last year of the previous period); each panel uses the same sample for all measures. A positive interaction means that better institutions mitigate the growth cost of government size. Panel C: annual panel 1996–2019 (the window of Mollick and Vianna, 2024), government consumption interacted with the mean of the six WGI indicators (centred), country and year fixed effects, lagged growth and the controls of Table 1, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3); this is the specification registered in the analysis plan, written when the published text was not available, and it is an approximate replication only. Panel D (exploratory, added after the plan when the text was obtained): the published specification of Mollick and Vianna (2024, Table 2), with growth of real GDP (%) on its lag, log government consumption, WGI government effectiveness, their interaction, log investment and population growth, country and year fixed effects, heteroskedasticity-robust (White) standard errors, their 138 countries; only the measure of government consumption changes across columns, on the same sample. Full results in Table S17. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
