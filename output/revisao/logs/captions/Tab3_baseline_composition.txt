Table 3. Baseline estimates and the composition of spending
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, five-year average), 1979–2023; all regressors in t_p − 1, the last year of the previous period. Predetermined controls: log GDP per capita, human capital, investment, openness, inflation, population growth and commodity terms-of-trade growth, all in t_p − 1. Column 1: preferred H1 specification (T3), country and period fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country. Column 2: two-step system GMM with lagged growth, the wage bill and initial income instrumented by lags 2–3 (lagged growth: lags 1–2), collapsed, other controls and period dummies as standard instruments; Windmeijer-corrected standard errors. Column 3: bias-corrected fixed effects (Breitung, Kripfganz and Hayakawa, 2022), which assumes the controls are strictly exogenous; robust standard errors. Columns 4–6: wage bill as a share of total expenditure (expense plus net investment in non-financial assets); column 4 holds total expenditure constant; columns 5–6 add the shares of goods and services, interest, other expense and either public investment (5) or transfers (6), total expenditure and revenue, so the wage-bill coefficient measures a reallocation of one percentage point of spending from the omitted category; the fiscal balance is implied. Shares, revenue and expenditure follow the extended cleaning rules (Table S1). Wild cluster bootstrap: Webb weights, 9,999 replications, clustered by country. Countries row: clusters (columns 1, 4–6) or groups (columns 2–3). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
