Table S14. Local projections by horizon: merit regimes, multiple-testing adjustment and alternative inference
Cumulative response of 100 × log GDP per capita from t − 1 to t + h to a real wage bill shock of one percentage point of lagged GDP (draft-7 specification), annual panel 1996–2023, country and year fixed effects, two lags of the shock and of growth. Merit regimes: merit above the sample median in the year before the shock. Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3). Romano-Wolf: step-down adjustment over the seven horizons of the high − low difference, 999 bootstrap resamples of countries (cluster-robust t statistics in the resamples). Lag-augmented local projections (Montiel Olea and Plagborg-Møller, 2021): one extra lag of the shock and of growth and heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors. Half-panel jackknife: 2 × full-sample estimate minus the average of the 1996–2009 and 2010–2023 estimates, a correction for the small-T fixed-effects bias; there is no Stata implementation of Herbst and Johannsen (2024), so their correction is not applied. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
