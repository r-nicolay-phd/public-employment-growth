Table 1. The wage bill coefficient under different measures of the wage bill
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%). Annual rows: panel 1996–2023, same sample in all rows (country-years with every measure), country and year fixed effects, lagged growth, lagged log GDP per capita, human capital, investment, openness, inflation, population growth and commodity terms-of-trade growth; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors (maximum lag 3), confidence intervals from the t distribution with T − 1 degrees of freedom. Trend GDP is the trend of log real GDP: Hamilton filter (H = 2, k = 2, predetermined), one-sided HP filter (lambda = 6.25, recursive, uses only data up to t) and two-sided HP filter (lambda = 6.25, uses future data; reported for comparison only). Five-year rows: non-overlapping averages 1979–2023, same sample in all rows, country and period fixed effects, controls of the conventional specification (log GDP per capita in t_p − 1, human capital in t_p and period means of investment, openness, inflation, population growth and commodity terms-of-trade growth), standard errors clustered by country. Tested differences: the six annual measures are stacked and estimated jointly with measure-specific controls and fixed effects (country × measure and year × measure); differences tested by wild cluster bootstrap clustered by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). The lagged measure and the Hamilton measure are not independent evidence: both use only information up to t − 1 or t − 2 (correlation between them in the annual sample: 0.965). Microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
