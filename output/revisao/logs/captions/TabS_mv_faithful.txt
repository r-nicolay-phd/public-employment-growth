Table S17. Mollick and Vianna (2024) with the published specification (exploratory)
Mollick and Vianna (2024, Public Choice 198: 189–207), equation (1) and Table 2: growth of real GDP (%, WDI NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG) on its own lag, log general government final consumption (% of GDP, NE.CON.GOVT.ZS), institutional quality (WGI government effectiveness GE, control of corruption CC or rule of law RL, in levels), the interaction of the last two, log gross capital formation (% of GDP, NE.GDI.TOTL.ZS) and population growth, with country and year fixed effects and heteroskedasticity-robust (White) standard errors in parentheses, on the 138 countries listed in their Table 1 (Puerto Rico is not in the project data). WDI series downloaded on 13 November 2026; WGI has no estimates for 1997, 1999 and 2001, which are linearly interpolated because the published number of observations implies that those years are included. Panel A: published coefficients and standard errors (stars from the normal distribution). Panels B and C: this replication; stars from the White standard errors; in panel C, p-values with standard errors clustered by country in brackets. The replication comes close to the published sample (3,113 against 3,141 observations with GE) and within R², and reproduces the signs and significance of the full-period estimates, but its interaction coefficients are 2.5 to 2.9 times the published ones; without the authors' code the difference cannot be traced (later vintages of the WDI series are one candidate). Panel C changes only the measure of government consumption (log of consumption over trend GDP, Hamilton filter; or consumption lagged one year) on the same country-years. The analysis was added after the registered plan, when the published text became available, and is exploratory. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
