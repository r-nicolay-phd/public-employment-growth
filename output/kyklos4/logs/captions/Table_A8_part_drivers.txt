Preferred five-year specification (wage bill in t_p − 1, controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country). The 44 country-periods are those of the 355 for which the previous period or the ratios in t_p − 2 and t_p − 3 are missing, so that they drop out of the common sample of the bias checks (311). First reporting period: the first period in which the country has the GFS wage bill in t_p − 1. Differences in means: regression on an indicator of the 44, standard errors clustered by country. Group coefficients: the wage bill interacted with the group indicator; wild cluster bootstrap p of the interaction (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). Share explained: [β(355) − β(355 without the block)] / [β(355) − β(311)]. Full-sample checks: growth before the period is the average growth of period p − 1 or, when that period is missing, the mean annual growth of the five years before t_p (13 country-periods recovered); each variant is compared with the preferred specification on the same observations. Simulated nulls: observed minus the mean of 1,000 simulations of a zero-effect wage bill (mean-reverting design; anchored design with unscaled residuals; anchored design with the moment-matched k of Table A4, panel H, at ε = 0.48); 95% CI from 999 country pairs-bootstrap draws, each re-estimating the observed coefficient and running one new simulation on the resampled countries. Transition economies (former Soviet Union, Central and Eastern Europe, Balkans), periods starting before 1999: 3 of the 355 country-periods (BGR, HUN and SVN 1994); blocks are dropped only among the 44 lost country-periods, so the transition block is one country-period (SVN 1994, N = 354), whereas Table A9 drops all three (N = 352).
