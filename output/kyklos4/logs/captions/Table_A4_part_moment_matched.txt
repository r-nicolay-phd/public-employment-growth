Panel K: anchored design (the synthetic ratio starts each year from the observed ratio of the previous year and moves with nominal GDP with elasticity ε), with the drawn residuals multiplied by k ∈ {0, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.75, 1}; 1,000 replications per cell. k* minimises the sum of the absolute deviations between simulated mean and observed value, each divided by its simulated standard deviation, of four moments: within-country SD of the ratio, its AR(1), the coefficient of the lagged ratio and the coefficient of the ratio over Hamilton trend GDP; the same-year coefficient does not enter. Share: simulated mean of the same-year coefficient over the observed one (−0.723). Reproduces both: the observed lagged and Hamilton coefficients lie inside the 2.5–97.5 percentiles of their simulated distributions at k*. Reference row k = 0: the synthetic ratio moves only with nominal GDP, so the simulation is deterministic and no percentiles are shown. Separate elasticities: Δln s = (ε_r − 1) Δln real GDP + (ε_p − 1) deflator inflation + k u, with the elasticities of panel M. Panel L: the numerator residual has a component ψ × real per-capita growth; k and ψ (grid −0.5 to 0.5) chosen by the same distance with 200 replications per cell, then 1,000 replications at the chosen point. Panel M: within-country and within-year variation in the Table 1 sample; A = −(1 − ε) Δln nominal GDP; implied share = cov(growth, A) / cov(growth, Δln s); elasticities from 100 × Δln W on 100 × Δln real GDP and deflator inflation, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors. Regressions in each replication as in Table 1 (controls of Table 1, country and year fixed effects). Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the fourth addendum).
