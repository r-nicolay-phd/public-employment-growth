Table A9 (continued). On a common scale the annual evidence is imprecise and compatible with the five-year estimates
Common object: the effect on average annual growth of real GDP per capita over t_p … t_p + 4 of a wage bill 1 point of GDP higher, maintained from t_p − 1. Annual: Table 1 model (country and year fixed effects, controls of Table 1) with the ratio in t − 1 to t − 5, or their average, on the country-years with five lags; implied effect (1/5) Σ_h e_h with e_h = Σ_{j ≤ h+1} β_j + ρ e_{h−1}, ρ the coefficient on lagged growth; Driscoll-Kraay standard errors and delta-method CI. Local projections: shock (a) of Table A16; average growth over h = 0–4 on the mean response of the wage bill ratio over h = 0–4, instrumented by the shock, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors; with a first-stage effective F below 10 the object is not identified and the Anderson-Rubin CI is reported (standard errors clustered by country). Five-year: preferred specification on 355 and on the common bias sample (311). WWBI: the preferred specification on the registered and strict H2 samples with the GFS wage bill in t_p − 1. Harmonised: the country-periods of the registered H2 sample that are also in the preferred sample, and the annual country-years of those periods. Tests: differences across estimates computed on the same 999 country pairs-bootstrap draws; the annual effect is also tested against −0.14 and −0.33 with its bootstrap standard error. Holm: family of wage-bill coefficients of Tables A9 (subsample rows), A10 (specification rows) and A15 (size panel); bootstrap p where reported. p-values and 95% CIs of the five-year and WWBI rows are from standard errors clustered by country (t distribution); the wild cluster bootstrap p-values of the same coefficients are 0.120 (preferred specification, Table A17), 0.030 and 0.036 (WWBI samples, Table A15). Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the fourth addendum).
