Table A6 (continued). Mollick and Cabral: the predetermined five-year coefficient is negative and smaller in absolute value than the period-mean one
Approximate replication of Mollick and Cabral (2011), five-year blocks: log GDP per capita at the end of the block on its lag, population growth, log investment and log government consumption / GDP measured in the last year of the previous block (t_p − 1) or as the block mean. Fixed effects: country fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country, wild cluster bootstrap CI and p (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). System GMM: two-step, Windmeijer-corrected standard errors, normal CI. Difference: the two measures stacked with measure-specific controls and country × measure fixed effects, wild cluster bootstrap. Panel B differs from these rows in the GMM step and in inference: its System GMM is one-step with robust standard errors (hence the small differences in the coefficients, e.g. −0.072 one-step against −0.068 two-step for the period mean in 1986–2023) and its fixed-effects CIs and stars come from standard errors clustered by country (e.g. t_p − 1 in 1986–2004: −0.078, clustered CI [−0.133; −0.024], wild bootstrap p = 0.081). Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the fourth addendum).
