Table A10. Heterogeneity by fiscal rules
Specification A1 (Table 2, column 1) with the wage bill interacted with an indicator of a numerical fiscal rule in force in the first year of the period (IMF Fiscal Rules Dataset, 1985-2024 update). The first coefficient is the wage bill effect without the rule; the row below the line is the effect with the rule in force (sum of the two coefficients). Rules cover national and supranational rules (EU Stability and Growth Pact, currency unions) for the central or general government; columns 4 and 5 count national rules only. IMF members absent from the dataset are coded as having no rule; column 6 restricts the sample to countries listed in the dataset; column 7 excludes countries that were EU members at any time in 1979-2023 (EU-27 and the United Kingdom). In column 8 the first interaction refers to the expenditure rule and the second to the budget balance rule, entered jointly, and the effect with the rule in force is evaluated with both rules in force. Column 9 adds the interaction with meritocratic recruitment of Table 4, column 1 (V-Dem, first year of the period); the effect with the rule in force is evaluated with a budget balance rule and merit at its sample mean. Rows on counts of rule periods and switching countries refer to the rule in columns 1-7. Five-year averages, country and period fixed effects, controls of Table 2, standard errors clustered by country; microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
