Table A6. Threshold models with meritocratic recruitment as the regime variable
Dependent variable: annual growth of real GDP per capita (%). Strongly balanced annual panels (required by both estimators), microstates excluded. Regime-dependent regressor: general government wage bill (% of GDP), lagged one year; threshold variable: V-Dem meritocratic recruitment (higher values = more merit-based), lagged one year. Columns 1-2: dynamic panel threshold model of Seo and Shin (2016), first-difference GMM (xthenreg), asymptotic standard errors; the lower-regime slope and the change in the slope above the threshold are shown. In column 1 the number of moment conditions (all available lags of growth for each year) exceeds the number of countries, which is the instrument proliferation that the pre-specified rule excludes; its estimates are reported for transparency and are not interpreted. Column 3: fixed-effects threshold model of Hansen (1999), xthreg, with year dummies as regime-independent regressors and no instruments; slopes by regime. Linearity tests by bootstrap with 500 replications. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
