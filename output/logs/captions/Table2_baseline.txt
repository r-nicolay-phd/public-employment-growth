Table 2. Public wage bill and growth: baseline estimates
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, annual average over the period in columns 1, 2 and 4-7; annual in column 3). The wage bill is compensation of employees of the general government (IMF GFS), measured in the first year of each period (columns 1, 2, 4-7) or lagged one year (column 3). Columns 1-2 and 4-5: country and period fixed effects, standard errors clustered by country. Column 3: annual panel with country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors; regressors lagged one year. Columns 4-5: government budget constraint, with expenditure shares of goods and services, interest, other expense and either transfers (4) or investment (5), total expenditure and revenue; the fiscal balance is implied. The wage bill coefficient measures a reallocation of one percentage point of spending from the omitted category to the wage bill. Column 6: two-step system GMM (Blundell-Bond) with Windmeijer-corrected standard errors; lagged growth, wage bill, investment and initial income instrumented with lags 2-3, collapsed. Column 7: bias-corrected fixed effects for dynamic panels (Breitung, Kripfganz and Hayakawa, 2022), robust standard errors. Five-year periods 1979-2023, three-year periods 1981-2023, annual 1996-2023; microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
