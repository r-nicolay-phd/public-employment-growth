Table 4. Does bureaucratic recruitment moderate the growth cost of the wage bill?
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, five-year average). Wage bill: general government compensation of employees (% of GDP) in the first year of the period. Meritocratic recruitment: V-Dem v2stcritrecadm (criteria for appointment decisions in the state administration; higher values = more merit-based), measured in the first year of the period. WGI: government effectiveness, first year of the period (from 1996). Columns 3-7 split the sample at the median and terciles of merit in the estimation sample of column 1; the equality test comes from a pooled model with all coefficients and the period effects interacted with the group. Controls as in Table 2. Country and period fixed effects; standard errors clustered by country. The threshold estimates (Seo and Shin, 2016) are in Table A6. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
