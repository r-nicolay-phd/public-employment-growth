Table 3. Employment or pay? Decomposing the public wage bill
Dependent variable: growth of real GDP per capita (%, five-year average). Public employment is public sector employment as a share of total employment (World Bank WWBI); the relative public wage is the general government wage bill (% of GDP, IMF GFS) divided by the public employment share, i.e. pay per public employee relative to GDP per worker; the public wage premium is the WWBI conditional public-private wage gap with respect to all private employees (log points). WWBI variables are survey-based: the first observation available within each period is used. Country and period fixed effects; standard errors clustered by country. Turning point = -b1/(2 b2), with a 95% confidence interval from the delta method. Five-year periods, 1999-2023 (WWBI starts in 2000); microstates excluded. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
