Table A8 (continued). On a common sample, every bias check stays within 0.05 of the preferred estimate (−0.019 to −0.001)
Replaces the bias panel of the earlier version. Common sample: the country-periods of the preferred specification (five-year averages, wage bill in t_p − 1, controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects) for which every variant can be estimated (output gap in t_p − 1, average growth of p − 1, and the annual ratios in t_p − 2 and t_p − 3). The negative-gap model is summarised by its average effect, the coefficient after a positive gap plus the interaction times the share of negative gaps (0.40). Bootstrap p: wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications; WRE for 2SLS). Differences from the preferred estimate: country pairs bootstrap, the same 999 draws for every variant. Null-adjusted estimates: observed coefficient minus the mean of 1,000 simulations of a zero-effect wage bill (mean-reverting design of Table A4, panel B, or calibrated anchored design of Table A4, panel E), with the simulated ratios replacing the observed ones (also in the instruments); the CI comes from the same pairs-bootstrap draws, each re-estimating the observed coefficient and running one new simulation on the resampled countries, so that it combines sampling and simulation error. Leave one period out: the preferred specification on its full sample, dropping each period in turn, clustered standard errors. Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the third plan addendum).
