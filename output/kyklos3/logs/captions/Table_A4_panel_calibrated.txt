Panels E and F: anchored design of panel A (the synthetic ratio starts each year from the observed ratio of the previous year and moves with nominal GDP with elasticity ε), with the drawn residuals multiplied by k. k is the value of a grid from 0.30 to 1.00 (step 0.05; 200 replications per value at ε = 0.48) whose synthetic ratio has the within-country SD (panel E) or the AR(1) coefficient (panel F) closest to the observed one (0.976 and 0.970). Both criteria select the lowest value of the grid, 0.30; with k = 0.30 the within-country SD matches the observed one, while the AR(1) coefficient (0.961) stays below the observed one. 1,000 replications per ε. Cells: mean of the simulated coefficients and, below, their 2.5–97.5 percentiles; regressions as in panel A (annual model of Table 1 with its controls, country and year fixed effects, Table 1 sample; five-year preferred specification with controls in t_p − 1, country and period fixed effects). ε = 0.48 is the elasticity of the nominal wage bill to nominal GDP estimated on 1997–2023 (1,774 country-years); ε = 0.52 is the same elasticity on 1996–2023 (1,814 country-years, Table A3); ε = 0.71 is the elasticity of the nominal wage bill to nominal GDP growth holding real growth fixed (panel D), i.e. its response to prices. Panel G: simulated mean of the same-year coefficient over the observed one (−0.723), by design; the placebo row is the coefficient of the fixed-numerator placebo ratio over the observed one.
