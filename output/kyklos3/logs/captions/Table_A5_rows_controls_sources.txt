Table A5 (continued). The same-year bias survives predetermined annual controls and growth measured from a different source
Predetermined annual controls: the annual model of Table 1 (1996–2023, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors) on the Table 1 sample with all controls also available in t − 1 (the same sample in the four blocks), with the controls of Table 1, with all controls in t − 1 (lagged growth, lagged log GDP per capita, human capital, investment, openness, inflation, population growth and terms-of-trade growth), and without inflation or investment. Different sources: growth of real GDP per capita from the Penn World Table 11.0 (100 × Δlog of rgdpna / pop; its own lag replaces lagged WDI growth among the controls), with the wage bill ratio built with IMF GDP; and WDI growth with the ratio of general government compensation of employees in national currency (IMF GFS) to nominal GDP of the IMF World Economic Outlook. The GFS ratios are computed with WEO GDP (correlation with the ratio of Table 1: 0.925), so the second check differs from Table 1 mainly by vintage; the Penn World Table check changes the same-year coefficient by 10%. Differences: the three measures stacked with measure-specific controls and year effects, wild cluster bootstrap by country (Webb weights, 9,999 replications). Exploratory (post-plan, pre-registered in the third plan addendum). * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
