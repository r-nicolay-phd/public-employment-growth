Table L1b. Government consumption x institutions: contemporaneous versus lagged measure, robustness
Coefficient (standard error) of the interaction between government consumption (% of GDP, WDI) and institutional quality, with government consumption measured in the same year as growth or lagged one year. Annual panel, country and year fixed effects, Driscoll-Kraay standard errors; the two columns of each row use the same sample. Institutions: mean of the six WGI indicators except in row 3. Row 4 uses only lagged log GDP per capita, investment, openness, inflation and population growth as controls. * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01.
