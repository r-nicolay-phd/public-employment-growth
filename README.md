# Public wage bills and economic growth

Project repository of the article "Accounting, Not Averaging Out: Public Wage Bills and Economic Growth" (Rodolfo Tomás da Fonseca Nicolay and coauthors; submitted to *Kyklos*).

**Pre-registration.** The analysis plan and its four addenda are deposited at the Open Science Framework: [https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/EF7K3](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/EF7K3).

## Where things are

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `registration_osf/` | The deposited plan and addenda (`.md` and `.pdf`), with the registration timeline |
| `docs/` | The plan (`PLANO_REVISAO.md`) and the addenda (`plan_addendum_kyklos*.md`) as committed, the registration timeline, the deviations from the plan and the logs of each round |
| `code/` | Estimation code: `revisao/` (registered plan) and `kyklos/`, `kyklos2/`, `kyklos3/`, `kyklos4/` (the four addenda) |
| `output/` | Tables, figures and memoranda of each round; the exhibits of version 5 are in `output/kyklos4/final/` |
| `replication_kyklos/` | Replication package (README with instructions; data are not versioned and are documented by the `SOURCE.txt` files) |

The commits of the plan and of the addenda keep their original hashes and dates (tags `analysis-plan-2026-10-17` and `kyklos-addendum-4`); see `docs/registration_timeline.md`.
